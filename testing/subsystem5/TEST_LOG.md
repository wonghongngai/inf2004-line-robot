*Method:* **auto** = `TEST_CHECK` in code, result printed on the console;
**manual** = a person observes the result (LED, scope, meter, phone app…).
*(planned)* = defined but not yet runnable.

*Scan rework (2026-11-05):* the scan range is narrowed to 50–130° (40° either side of forward; was 30–150°). Coarse (FR5-1): the servo jumps to 50, 70, 90, 110 and 130 in turn and pings once at each, after `servo_settle_ms()`, pausing `COARSE_DWELL_MS` before the next jump. Fine (FR5-2): it glides smoothly from one coarse step before the first coarse angle that saw the obstacle to one step after the last, pinging every 5° (S5-U28). The glide is time-based (position from the 1 MHz timer), keeps the servo updated while each echo is timed, and its speeds keep every 20 ms pulse above the SG90 10 µs dead band (S5-U21); before this the fine scan stepped point to point and was jerky. The NFR5-2 (coarse ≤ 600 ms) and NFR5-3 (detection → decision ≤ 2 s) limits are dropped for smoothness. **S5-U05, U06 and U15 no longer apply** (U05: the coarse scan is now 50/70/90/110/130; U15's window rule and 15-point cut are replaced by U28); their old results are kept below as history. S5-U11, U12 and U16 still apply.

### Hump capture procedure (#27 step 2; diagnosis, not a scored test)

Why: in the 21 Nov `integrated` runs only 2 of 6 hump passes gave an `EVENT HUMP`; the rest ended in `hump timed out`. This capture records what the detector saw on every pass so the thresholds can be set from data (step 3) before S4-U09/U10 are scored.

The image is `sh build.sh integrated S4_TRACE=1` → `robot-integrated-s4trace.uf2`. It behaves like the normal `integrated` build except for logging: every IMU sample goes into a RAM ring, and when a hump measurement ends (`event`, `timeout`, or `lift`) the last 128 samples (2.56 s) are printed four per line, one line per 20 ms cycle, so nothing is printed while the hump is being measured. While moving on level ground with the detector armed it also prints `[S4:IMU] HT noise n=50 f=min..max |f|=mean raw=min..max` once per second (driving noise for S4-U08). The per-sample `HUMP ACTIVE` and `HUMP DEBUG` lines are left out of this image. **Not for the demo.**

Line format (columns are named in each dump's header line):

```
[S4:IMU] HT#k <event|timeout|lift|lift (hump abandoned)> len=N cols=n,ph,mv,raw,f,d,h,s,cyc
[S4:IMU] HT#k n,ph,mv,raw,f,d,h,s,cyc;n,ph,...      (oldest first)
```

`ph` 0 idle, 1 first slope, 2 opposite slope, 3 settling, 8 not armed, 9 lifted; `raw`/`f` forward dy and its 3-sample average in mg (`f` is the lift deviation when `ph`=9); `d` wheel distance that sample in mm; `h` peak height so far in mm; `s` settle count; `cyc` the previous cycle time in ms.

1. Measure the ramp with a ruler (height at the top, its length along the line, and which side of the robot it goes under) and write the values down before starting.
2. Flash `robot-integrated-s4trace.uf2`; USB console logging to a file; MQTT dashboard connected. Keep the robot still for the first 1.5 s after boot (calibration).
3. Flat floor first: START, let it follow a straight stretch with no hump for at least 10 s, PAUSE. Twice. (Gives the `HT noise` lines.)
4. The ramp, 3 passes: reboot, START about 30 cm before the hump, let it drive over and 50 cm past, PAUSE. **Write down which boot was which pass** (time of boot or a sticky note per pass).
5. Once: START, then lift the robot while it follows (as S4-U12 (a)); this checks the `lift` dump.
6. Save the log as `testing/subsystem4/logs/2026-11-DD_hump_capture_S4.txt` with a header saying the commit, the image and the pass list, and record a row in Results (INCOMPLETE/RECORDED: diagnosis only).

What to look for afterwards (step 3): on timed-out passes, whether `f` never stays within ±40 mg (`S4_HUMP_EXIT_FLAT_MG`) for 4 samples on the top or after the hump (vibration), or whether the climb itself lasts longer than 75 samples (`S4_HUMP_TIMEOUT_SAMPLES`, 1.5 s) at the `integrated` cruise speed; on detected passes, whether the event fired at the **top** of the hump rather than after the descent (a host trace on 23 Nov showed the detector accepts "up slope → flat" without waiting for the down slope, so a hump with a flat top is reported before it ends); and `cyc` above 20 ms anywhere.
